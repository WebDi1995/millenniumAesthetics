Our Specialty

Prosthodontics: rebuilding what's missing.

Prosthodontics is the science of replacing missing or damaged teeth so the bite, jaw, and facial structure keep working the way they should.

P1

Crowns & Bridges

Restore a badly damaged tooth, or replace one to three missing teeth without surgery.

P2

Partial Dentures

Removable appliances that replace several missing teeth while preserving the rest.

P3

Complete Dentures

Full-arch replacement for patients missing all upper or lower teeth.

Our Specialty

Dental Implants: a permanent foundation.

A titanium fixture placed in the jawbone acts as an artificial tooth root — giving crowns, bridges, and dentures a stable, bone-anchored foundation instead of relying on neighboring teeth or suction alone.

I1

Single-Tooth Implants

Replace one missing tooth without touching the teeth beside it.

I2

Implant-Supported Bridges

Replace several missing teeth in a row using just two or more implants.

I3

Full-Arch (All-on-4/6)

A fixed, full set of teeth anchored on four to six implants — for patients missing most or all of their teeth.
