About Millennium Aesthetics

Built by clinicians who specialize in the hard cases.

Millennium Aesthetics was founded in Kathmandu to give patients access to advanced prosthodontic and implant care, without needing to travel abroad for it.

Our Story

Why we chose to specialize.

[Placeholder] Millennium Aesthetics opened with a simple observation: patients who lost teeth to decay, injury, or age often faced a choice between a removable denture that never quite fit, or traveling abroad for implant care. We built a clinic focused on closing that gap locally — combining general dental care with deep, ongoing training in prosthodontics and implantology.

Today, general and family dentistry keep the practice grounded in everyday care, while our prosthodontics and implant work remains the specialty patients travel across the valley — and occasionally the country — to access.
